This Privacy Policy describes how Petualangan Bleki Oyen & Mochi ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Third-Party Services

The Game does not require users to create an account or directly provide personal information such as names or addresses. However, the Game uses trusted third-party services that may collect identifiers used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect Device/Advertising IDs and app interaction data to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.

2. Data Security

All data transmitted by our third-party SDKs (Google AdMob and Google Play Services) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS protocols.

3. Children's Privacy

Our services do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take necessary action.
